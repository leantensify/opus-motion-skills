# Generative Motion — Timing

| # | Pattern | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Assembly | 1.0–2.5s depending on particle count and target complexity. |
| 02 | Readable hold | Once text forms, reduce velocity and hold at least 0.8s. |
| 03 | Explosion | Fast 0.2–0.6s release, slower 0.8–1.8s reformation. |
| 04 | Determinism | All randomness must be seeded so exact frames can be reproduced. |

## General rules
Establish the system before escalating it. Let one major event dominate each beat. Hold important copy after motion settles. Review once with audio muted.
