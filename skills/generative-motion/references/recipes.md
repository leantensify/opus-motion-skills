# Generative Motion — Shot & Transition Recipes

## 01 · Generative finale

Free flow → phrase assembly → hold → ring → play icon → final statement → collapse point.

## 02 · Identity loop

Particles form logo, disperse along field, then return to identical initial logo state.

## 03 · HUD-to-generative

HUD grid points detach; vector field bends them; they assemble into the next title.

## 04 · Data-to-art

Data points preserve identity but gain procedural forces; final spatial arrangement still reflects source categories.

## 05 · Short hook

Begin with partial recognizable formation already underway; complete target inside first 1–2 seconds.

## 06 · Quiet ending

After dense procedural movement, collapse to one point and hold silence/negative space.

## Handoff rule

Reuse an existing camera target, ring, grid, field, point set or geometry as the first element of the next scene whenever possible.
