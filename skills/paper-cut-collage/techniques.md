# Paper Cut / Collage — Techniques

| # | Technique | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Ransom Build | Place mixed paper-letter cutouts one by one with imperfect registration. |
| 02 | Tape Pin | Attach a label with animated translucent tape. |
| 03 | Paper Slide | Move a physical layer with stepped frames and visible shadow. |
| 04 | Scissor Reveal | Trace a cut path, then separate the paper along that contour. |
| 05 | Stop-Motion Jiggle | Alternate 2–4 hand-offset states at 8–12fps. |
| 06 | Layer Peel | Lift one corner with changing shadow, then reveal content beneath. |
| 07 | Stamp Hit | Apply ink stamp or label with fast scale impact and slight rotation. |
| 08 | Torn Wipe | Use a jagged paper edge as the transition boundary. |
| 09 | Photo Pinboard | Drop photos or screenshots with tape/pins and editorial captions. |
| 10 | Paper Disc | Use a textured circle as a recurring physical motif. |

## Combination rule
Use 2–4 primary techniques in a short scene. Repetition should create a system; adding every technique creates noise.

## QA
- Can the style be recognized with color removed?
- Is motion driven by the style's engine?
- Does at least one technique support the message directly?
