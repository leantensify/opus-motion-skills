# Paper Cut / Collage — Shot & Transition Recipes

## 01 · Editorial opener

Background sheet → headline scraps → photo crop → tape → one stamped accent.

## 02 · Fashion montage

Swap image crops as physical sheets while type remains pinned in place.

## 03 · Quote card

Build the quote from 2–3 paper textures, not one scrap per word; save chaos for emphasis.

## 04 · Paper-to-pixel

Freeze a hero collage, posterize it into square cells, then transition cells into a game grid.

## 05 · Looping desk

Elements slide in, are taped, then pulled off in reverse by an unseen hand for a seamless reset.

## 06 · End card

Reduce to one clean paper card with logo/CTA; retain one taped or torn detail.

## Handoff rule

Prefer transforming an existing material, edge, object, grid, line or texture into the first meaningful element of the next scene.
