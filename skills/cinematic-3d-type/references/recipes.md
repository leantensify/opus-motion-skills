# Cinematic 3D Type — Shot & Transition Recipes

## 01 · Premium reveal

Dark horizon → forms rise → slow orbit → light sweep → face-on hero.

## 02 · Camera-as-edit

Start close on one letter detail; pull out to title; fly through counter into next scene.

## 03 · Product/title combo

Product silhouette and title share depth; camera passes product edge to reveal type.

## 04 · Retro-to-3D

CRT line becomes horizon; fog opens depth; extruded letters rise from that same line.

## 05 · 3D-to-HUD

Camera flies into a ring/counter; ring becomes HUD reticle at the exact center.

## 06 · Finale hero

Use only one major camera move, then stop motion for a clean premium hold.

## Handoff rule

Reuse an existing camera target, ring, grid, field, point set or geometry as the first element of the next scene whenever possible.
