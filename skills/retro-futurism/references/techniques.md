# Retro Futurism — Signature Techniques

## 01 · CRT Power-On

Frame expands from a bright horizontal line into full image.

**Implementation:** Animate vertical scale from near-zero with a brief bloom.

## 02 · CRT Power-Off

Image compresses vertically to a line, then to a point.

**Implementation:** Use as a strong exit into 3D horizon or black.

## 03 · Scanline Sweep

A bright scan band moves through the image revealing or refreshing content.

**Implementation:** Keep the band narrow and repeat only when meaningful.

## 04 · VHS Tracking

Horizontal bands shift laterally for a brief signal disturbance.

**Implementation:** Localize the glitch in time and vertical region.

## 05 · RGB Split

Color channels offset during impact or tuning.

**Implementation:** Return quickly to alignment.

## 06 · Striped Sun

Horizontal bands build a circular sun or disc.

**Implementation:** Use masks and consistent stripe spacing.

## 07 · Tape Counter

Monospaced digits advance like deck timecode.

**Implementation:** Make values plausible and rhythmically useful.

## 08 · Chrome Type Sweep

A highlight travels across metallic or gradient lettering.

**Implementation:** Use the sweep as a hero moment, not continuous shine.

## 09 · Orbit Diagram

Simple rings/points rotate like a vintage science graphic.

**Implementation:** Use restrained line work and period labels.

## 10 · Tape Key Press

PLAY/REC/PAUSE control depresses and changes signal state.

**Implementation:** Tie button state to the next animation.

## 11 · Channel Tune

Content cycles through 2–3 visual states as if tuning channels.

**Implementation:** Use static/noise only during transition.

## 12 · Horizon Handoff

A bright CRT line becomes a perspective horizon for cinematic 3D.

**Implementation:** Match line position and luminance across scenes.

## Selection rule

Use 2–4 techniques for most short scenes. A recognizable system is stronger than a catalogue of effects.
