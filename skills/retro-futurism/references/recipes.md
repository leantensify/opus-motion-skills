# Retro Futurism — Shot & Transition Recipes

## 01 · Technology-history beat

CRT power-on → period title → orbit/diagram motion → brief tracking glitch → clean hold.

## 02 · Music visualizer

Striped sun and chrome type establish composition; signal events react at structural music moments.

## 03 · Retro product ad

Tape key press reveals product; counter advances through three features; final channel locks clean.

## 04 · Retro-to-3D

Power-off collapses frame to horizontal line; line becomes 3D horizon and camera space opens.

## 05 · Looping signal

Channel tune cycles through three states and returns via static burst to exact initial frame.

## 06 · End card

Reduce effects, stabilize signal, hold title/CTA with subtle scanlines only.

## Handoff rule

Prefer transforming an existing material, edge, object, grid, line or texture into the first meaningful element of the next scene.
