# Retro Futurism — Timing

| # | Pattern | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Power-on/off | 0.25–0.6s plus a brief bloom/afterimage. |
| 02 | Tracking glitch | 2–8 frames; never leave continuously active. |
| 03 | Title hero | 1.0–2.0s readable hold after reveal. |
| 04 | Tape motion | Use constant speed for counters/reels to create mechanical contrast. |

## General rules
Establish the system before escalating it. Let one major event dominate each beat. Hold important copy after motion settles. Review once with audio muted.
