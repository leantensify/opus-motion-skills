# Swiss / International — Shot & Transition Recipes

## 01 · Editorial opener

Grid appears subtly → index and rule lock in → headline slides along columns → circle lands last.

## 02 · Data-to-editorial

Chart bars flatten into grid modules; labels reflow into a poster-style summary.

## 03 · Premium product section

Large product crop occupies 7–8 columns; type moves in remaining columns; accent circle marks the active feature.

## 04 · Section transition

Extend one rule to full height, wipe across, then reveal the next grid on the same axis.

## 05 · Explainer sequence

One module changes per beat while the master grid remains stable, preserving orientation.

## 06 · End card

Collapse modules into a simple logo/headline/URL composition with generous white space.

## Transition checklist

Before using a cut, ask whether an existing object can become:
- the next scene's mask
- a grid/rule/axis
- a circle/ring/portal
- a block/bar/module
- a texture or background field
- the camera target
- the first letter or shape of the next composition

A cut is appropriate when discontinuity is the point. Otherwise prefer a motivated handoff.
