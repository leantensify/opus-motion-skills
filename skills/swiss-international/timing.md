# Swiss / International — Timing

| # | Pattern | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Poster assembly | 1–2s establish grid, 2–4s place type, 4–6s add geometry, 6–8s hero. |
| 02 | Minimal beat | One significant movement every 0.5–1.0s; leave intentional stillness. |
| 03 | Rotation | Quarter-turn should complete in roughly 8–14 frames at 30fps, then settle without bounce. |
| 04 | Readability | Hold small informational copy longer than expressive display type. |

## General timing rules

- Establish the visual system before escalating it.
- One major event should dominate each beat.
- Readable information needs a hold after motion settles.
- Anticipation should be shorter than the main action unless suspense is intentional.
- Do not use spring physics everywhere; choose easing to match material and style.
- Review the animation once with audio muted.
