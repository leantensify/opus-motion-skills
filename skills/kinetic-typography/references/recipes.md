# Kinetic Typography — Shot & Transition Recipes

## 01 · 2-second hook

Start with silence/empty frame → one oversized word slams in → second word collides → final phrase locks readable by ~1.6s.

## 02 · Manifesto build

Reveal one phrase per beat; reuse the previous phrase as background texture; end with all phrases aligning into one statement.

## 03 · Quote emphasis

Keep most of the sentence restrained; animate only 1–3 semantic words with scale/tracking transformations.

## 04 · Logo handoff

Use a circular counter or letter stroke to become the first geometry of the logo/end card.

## 05 · Beat drop

Pre-drop: tight monochrome type. Drop: sudden full-frame scale and repeated rows. Resolve: one clean hero line.

## 06 · Vertical social

Use 2–3 stacked type zones, large safe-area margins, and fast re-composition rather than horizontal travel.

## Transition checklist

Before using a cut, ask whether an existing object can become:
- the next scene's mask
- a grid/rule/axis
- a circle/ring/portal
- a block/bar/module
- a texture or background field
- the camera target
- the first letter or shape of the next composition

A cut is appropriate when discontinuity is the point. Otherwise prefer a motivated handoff.
