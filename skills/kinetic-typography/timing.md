# Kinetic Typography — Timing

| # | Pattern | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Hook slam | 0.0–0.5s anticipation, 0.5–1.1s impact, 1.1–1.8s readable hold. |
| 02 | Three-word build | Give each word 0.6–1.0s, then hold the complete phrase for at least 1.0s. |
| 03 | Beat-driven loop | Use one major typographic state change per beat or half-bar; avoid animating every glyph continuously. |
| 04 | Hero lockup | Reserve the final 20–30% of the scene for the cleanest composition. |

## General timing rules

- Establish the visual system before escalating it.
- One major event should dominate each beat.
- Readable information needs a hold after motion settles.
- Anticipation should be shorter than the main action unless suspense is intentional.
- Do not use spring physics everywhere; choose easing to match material and style.
- Review the animation once with audio muted.
