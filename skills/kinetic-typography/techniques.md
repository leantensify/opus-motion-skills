# Kinetic Typography — Techniques

These are reusable building blocks, not mandatory effects. Pick the smallest set that makes the brief clearer.

| # | Technique | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Word Slam | Drive a word from off-canvas or extreme scale into a locked baseline; use overshoot only once, then settle hard. |
| 02 | Tracking Explosion | Animate letter-spacing from compressed overlap to wide tracking, then snap to the final word shape. |
| 03 | Split Reveal | Slice a word horizontally or vertically and offset halves before rejoining on the beat. |
| 04 | Type Tunnel | Repeat the same word in depth or scale so the camera appears to travel through typography. |
| 05 | Baseline March | Move repeated words on shared baselines at different speeds while preserving alignment. |
| 06 | Letter Cascade | Release glyphs one-by-one with controlled delay, then resolve into a single phrase. |
| 07 | Mask Wipe | Use one word or glyph as a moving mask that reveals the next typographic state. |
| 08 | Outline-to-Fill | Transition from stroked letterforms to solid fill at the semantic or musical payoff. |
| 09 | Glyph Morph | Transform a structurally similar glyph into a shape, icon, or another letter rather than cutting away. |
| 10 | Type Wall | Build a dense repeated-text field, then isolate one instance as the hero message. |

## Combination rule

Use 2–4 primary techniques in a short scene. Repetition should create a system; adding every technique creates noise.

## QA

- Can the scene still be identified as Kinetic Typography with the color removed?
- Is the motion driven by the style's engine rather than generic transforms?
- Does at least one technique directly support the message?
