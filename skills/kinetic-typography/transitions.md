# Kinetic Typography — Transition Recipes

A transition should inherit something from the previous shot and hand something useful to the next one.

| # | Recipe | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | I → Rule | Stretch a vertical glyph into a rule that becomes the next scene's grid or divider. |
| 02 | O → Circle | Promote a circular glyph into a standalone disc/ring used by the next visual system. |
| 03 | Word → Mask | Scale a word until its counter or letterform becomes a viewport into the next scene. |
| 04 | Rows → Data | Convert repeated text rows into bars, tracks, or chart lines. |
| 05 | Crop → Hard Cut | Let an oversized glyph fully cover frame, then cut behind it. |
| 06 | Tracking → Particles | Break widened letter spacing into individual glyph fragments or particles. |

## Transition test

A good hand-off can be described as: **this existing object becomes that next object**. If the explanation is only "fade to the next scene", look for a stronger transformation.
