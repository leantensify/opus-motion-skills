# Blueprint — Shot & Transition Recipes

## 01 · Object build

Grid → centerlines → outline → dimensions → labels → hatch → final heavy stroke.

## 02 · Manufacturing story

Exploded components → part numbers → assembly arrows → finished product outline.

## 03 · Metric transition

Dimension number enlarges and becomes the next scene's headline or terminal output.

## 04 · Architecture opener

Title block and drawing number appear first, then plan lines construct from primary axes outward.

## 05 · Feature explanation

One callout at a time; isolate feature with heavier line weight while other geometry stays faint.

## 06 · End frame

Remove most construction guides but retain enough drafting language to preserve style identity.

## Transition checklist

Before using a cut, ask whether an existing object can become:
- the next scene's mask
- a grid/rule/axis
- a circle/ring/portal
- a block/bar/module
- a texture or background field
- the camera target
- the first letter or shape of the next composition

A cut is appropriate when discontinuity is the point. Otherwise prefer a motivated handoff.
