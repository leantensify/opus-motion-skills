# Blueprint — Signature Techniques

Use these as a vocabulary, not a checklist. Select the smallest combination that produces a distinct scene.

## 01 · Compass Circle

Construct a circle from center mark, radius line and animated arc.

**Implementation:** Draw centerlines first, then arc, then dimension.

## 02 · Dimension Reveal

Add extension lines, arrows and a measured label after geometry appears.

**Implementation:** Keep dimensions outside the primary object when possible.

## 03 · Exploded Assembly

Separate components along one axis, label them, then reassemble.

**Implementation:** Use consistent spacing and numbered callouts.

## 04 · Hatch Fill

Fill a closed shape with diagonal section hatching after outline completion.

**Implementation:** Clip repeated lines to the shape.

## 05 · Leader Annotation

A leader line grows to a point and reveals a concise callout.

**Implementation:** Avoid crossing other leaders.

## 06 · Coordinate Plot

Mark points with X/Y coordinates before connecting them into the final form.

**Implementation:** Animate points first, then line segments.

## 07 · Construction Ghosts

Keep faint guide circles/lines visible beneath the final bold geometry.

**Implementation:** Reduce opacity but do not erase all evidence of construction.

## 08 · Title Block

Use a drawing title block as metadata and scene index.

**Implementation:** Animate fields like REV, DWG, SCALE or DATE as content changes.

## 09 · Section Cut

A cut line crosses the object; the view transforms into a sectional diagram.

**Implementation:** Reuse the cut axis for transition.

## 10 · Tolerance Tick

Add small measurement changes or tolerance values to communicate precision.

**Implementation:** Use only when semantically relevant.

## 11 · Trace-to-Solid

Drafted outlines gain heavier final strokes or subtle fill after validation.

**Implementation:** Make this the payoff rather than fading to a completely unrelated style.

## 12 · Sheet Pan

Move across one large drafting sheet instead of cutting between isolated scenes.

**Implementation:** Keep grid and title block consistent to maintain orientation.

## Selection rule

For a short scene, 2–4 techniques are usually enough. If more than five are active simultaneously, simplify unless the deliberate goal is controlled overload.
