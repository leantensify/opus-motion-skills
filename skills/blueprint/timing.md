# Blueprint — Timing

| # | Pattern | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Construction sequence | Guide → outline → measurement → fill → approval. |
| 02 | Stroke speed | Primary geometry: 0.5–1.5s; secondary annotations: 0.2–0.6s. |
| 03 | Exploded view | Move components slowly enough to perceive relationship; 1.0–2.0s. |
| 04 | Hero hold | Once construction is complete, stop adding lines and hold the finished drawing. |

## General timing rules

- Establish the visual system before escalating it.
- One major event should dominate each beat.
- Readable information needs a hold after motion settles.
- Anticipation should be shorter than the main action unless suspense is intentional.
- Do not use spring physics everywhere; choose easing to match material and style.
- Review the animation once with audio muted.
