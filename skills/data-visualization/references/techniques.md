# Data Visualization — Signature Techniques

Use these as a vocabulary, not a checklist.

## 01 · Bar Re-rank

Bars move to new rank positions while values count to new states.

**Implementation:** Keep each category identity consistent.

## 02 · Bar-to-Scatter

Bars shrink to points positioned on x/y axes.

**Implementation:** Map each bar center to its new point coordinate.

## 03 · Outlier Focus

Most marks recede while one outlier enlarges and gains annotation.

**Implementation:** Use scale and opacity carefully without implying changed value.

## 04 · Line Draw

Reveal a time series along x-axis progression.

**Implementation:** Use stroke-dashoffset or sampled segments; align a playhead to the reveal.

## 05 · Scrubber Story

A timeline handle moves while all marks update to the selected time.

**Implementation:** Show date/value feedback near the handle.

## 06 · Stack-to-Group

Stacked bars separate into grouped bars to reveal composition.

**Implementation:** Maintain category ordering.

## 07 · Filter Collapse

Non-selected marks recede while selected marks reflow.

**Implementation:** Indicate filter state explicitly.

## 08 · Number Continuity

A headline number counts while the related visual mark changes.

**Implementation:** Use consistent rounding and suffix formatting.

## 09 · Small-Multiple Build

One chart duplicates into several comparable panels.

**Implementation:** Lock axes when comparison requires it.

## 10 · Axis Reframe

Change domain or scale with explicit cue rather than silently rescaling.

**Implementation:** Animate axis ticks and marks together.

## 11 · Map-to-Chart

Geographic marks move into ranked bars or dot plot positions.

**Implementation:** Use matching labels or identifiers.

## 12 · Chart-to-Geometry

Use bars, points or arcs as shapes for the next design style.

**Implementation:** Preserve their origin long enough that the transformation is understood.

## Selection rule

For a short scene, 2–4 techniques are usually enough. Simplify when technique count starts competing with the message.
