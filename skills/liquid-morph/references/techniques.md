# Liquid Morph — Signature Techniques

Use these as a vocabulary, not a checklist.

## 01 · Metaball Merge

Two or more blobs attract and join into one silhouette.

**Implementation:** Use SVG gooey filter, SDF or shader thresholding; animate centers with overlap.

## 02 · Blob Split

One mass stretches into a neck then separates into smaller masses.

**Implementation:** Delay separation until a visible bridge forms.

## 03 · Shape Absorb

A moving blob envelops an icon or word and exits carrying its cue.

**Implementation:** Use masking so absorption feels physical.

## 04 · Drip Type

Letters descend or distort as viscous forms before settling readable.

**Implementation:** Limit distortion near final hold.

## 05 · Liquid Wipe

A blob expands across the frame and becomes the next scene background.

**Implementation:** Use edge irregularity but keep wipe direction intentional.

## 06 · Orbit Merge

Small blobs orbit a center, collide, then form a symbol.

**Implementation:** Use orbital phase to time the merge.

## 07 · Elastic Ring

A circle or ring deforms under pressure then snaps back.

**Implementation:** Maintain approximate area to preserve material feel.

## 08 · Pour Transition

The whole composition flows downward or sideways into a new container.

**Implementation:** Use gravity direction consistently.

## 09 · Bubble Release

Small bubbles detach from a larger form and become particles or UI dots.

**Implementation:** Vary size slightly, not randomly across all properties.

## 10 · Liquid Mask Text

A moving fluid edge reveals typography through a mask.

**Implementation:** Hold the text after reveal.

## 11 · Viscosity Shift

Same path behaves differently as material changes from thick to thin.

**Implementation:** Change damping and speed, not visual noise alone.

## 12 · Freeze Frame

Fluid motion slows, surface stabilizes, and the blob becomes paper or solid geometry for handoff.

**Implementation:** Use texture or color change at the moment motion stops.

## Selection rule

For a short scene, 2–4 techniques are usually enough. Simplify when technique count starts competing with the message.
