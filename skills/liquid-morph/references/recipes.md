# Liquid Morph — Shot & Transition Recipes

## 01 · Logo morph

Blob → split → two forms recombine into logo silhouette → stable brand hold.

## 02 · Music loop

Orbiting blobs repeatedly merge and split on musical phrases; design reset so loop is seamless.

## 03 · Type transformation

Word emerges from liquid mask, holds readable, then letters soften and pour into next shape.

## 04 · Liquid-to-paper

Blob flattens and freeze-stops, texture appears, shadow separates it from background as paper disc.

## 05 · Product transition

Use one blob as a lens or mask that travels across three product views.

## 06 · Short hook

Single unexpected transformation in first second; avoid spending the hook on slow wobble.

## Transition checklist

Before using a cut, ask whether an existing object can become the next scene's mask, grid, axis, circle, block, texture, camera target, or first glyph.
