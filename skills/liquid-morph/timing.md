# Liquid Morph — Timing

| # | Pattern | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Merge | 0.6–1.2s, with slower final surface settling. |
| 02 | Split | Fast tension build, quick release, longer damping tail. |
| 03 | Wobble | 2–4 decreasing oscillations; stop before it becomes decorative noise. |
| 04 | Hero | Allow the final liquid shape at least 0.7s of relative stillness. |

## General rules
Establish the system before escalating it. Let one major event dominate each beat. Hold important copy after motion settles. Review once with audio muted.
