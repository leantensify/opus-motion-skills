# Pixel Art — Shot & Transition Recipes

## 01 · Stage intro

STAGE label → world scrolls in → sprite spawns → objective appears.

## 02 · Feature journey

Each product benefit becomes an obstacle, pickup or checkpoint rather than a text card.

## 03 · Coin CTA

Collect a branded coin/token; score becomes the CTA or product name.

## 04 · Pixel-to-sketch

Pixel blocks scatter into dots; dots connect into pencil construction marks.

## 05 · Seamless loop

Sprite exits right while identical world segment enters left, preserving cycle.

## 06 · Short hook

Begin with immediate movement or pickup; do not spend first seconds on a static retro title.

## Handoff rule

Prefer transforming an existing material, edge, object, grid, line or texture into the first meaningful element of the next scene.
